  구현:
  - QualityScore dataclass:
    contract_score: float (0~10), factuality_score: float (0~10),
    role_score: float (0~10), visual_score: float (0~10),
    total: float (가중합), breakdown: dict, violations: list[str]
  - calculate_score(contract_result, title_body_result, factuality_result,
                     dedup_result, role_result, html_result) -> QualityScore:
    contract = (contract_result.score * 0.5 + title_body_result.score * 0.5) * 10
    factuality = factuality_result.score * 10
    role = (dedup_result에서 유사도 감점 + role_result 충족도) * 10
    visual = html_result 통과 여부 * 10 (violations 있으면 감점)
    total = contract * 0.40 + factuality * 0.25 + role * 0.20 + visual * 0.15
  </action>
  <verify>python -c "from quality.scorer import QualityScore; print(QualityScore.__dataclass_fields__.keys())"</verify>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - QualityScore에 total, breakdown, violations 필드 존재
    - 모든 결과 만점 입력 시 total == 10.0
    - 사실성 0점 입력 시 total이 7.5 이하
  </acceptance_criteria>
